Real-Time Fraud Prevention Protocol

When a financial institution lodges a mortgage on this portal:

  • Automated Double-Mortgage Interceptor: Instantly cross-checks against all commercial banks, NBFCs, and housing finance companies to stop multiple loans on the same title deeds.
  • Sub-Registrar Lock: Automatically places a temporary caveat on the property in the DORIS Sub-Registrar registry so that the seller cannot execute an illegal sale deed while the mortgage is active.
  • CERSAI Direct Linkage: Issues a verifiable Central Registry of Securitisation asset ID with digital cryptographic timestamping.

Project Synopsis & Architecture Overview

National Capital Territory of Delhi Unified Land Ownership & Encumbrance Information System

Problem Statement & Objectives

The Government of NCT of Delhi requires a single-window portal that resolves the historical fragmentation between urban land records (MCD/DDA/L&DO), rural revenue records (Bhulekh Delhi / Record of Rights), executed deeds (DORIS Sub-Registrar database), and multi-platform encumbrances (CERSAI, Commercial Banks, Debt Recovery Tribunals, High Court injunctions, and Land Acquisition notifications).

1. Integrated Records

Seamlessly unites urban plotted UPIC records with rural Bigha-Biswa Khasra/Khatauni parcels across all 11 Delhi Districts.

2. Multi-Platform Encumbrances

Consolidates CERSAI mortgage registries, bank liens, High Court stays, DRT cases, and MCD tax default attachments into an exhaustive database.

3. Standard Input Search

Enables instant discovery using any standard identifier: UPIC, D-PIN, Khasra No., Deed Reg No., CERSAI ID, Owner Name, or Locality.
